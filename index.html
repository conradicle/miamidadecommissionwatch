Why this exists

Local government is public. It just isn't easy to follow.

BCC agendas run hundreds of pages and are written in procedural language. Almost nobody outside of government and lobbyists reads them cover to cover, but the decisions inside them affect rent, roads, permits, and public services across the county.

We summarize what matters, link back to the original source for every item, and stay out of the business of telling you what to think about it.
