Explainers and student perspectives

Two different kinds of writing live here: nonpartisan Explainers that go deeper on a single issue, and opinion Editorials written by Miami-Dade County students. They're kept clearly separate — see our editorial policy.

Student Editorials

Opinion pieces written by Miami-Dade County students about the Board of County Commissioners, constitutional offices, and county legislation. Unlike the Explainers above, these are opinion, not nonpartisan reporting — and they belong to their authors, not to us.

Submit your editorial ↗
These are opinions, not our reporting. Every editorial here is written and submitted by a Miami-Dade County student and is credited by name and school. It reflects that student's own views, not the views of Miami-Dade Commission Watch. Editorials are reviewed before publishing but are not fact-checked line by line the way our meeting summaries are.

Miami-Dade Commission Watch is an independent, student-run project founded by Conrad Gelber and Daniel Llobet. It is not affiliated with, operated by, or endorsed by Miami-Dade County government. Spanish and Haitian Creole translations on this site are generated automatically by Google Translate and are not reviewed by us. They may contain errors, especially for legal and government terms. When in doubt, refer to the English original.
