What you're signing up for

How it works

You get your own site

Your city gets its own address, yourcity.miamidadecommissionwatch.com, with its own officials directory, meeting summaries, map and public comment guide.

You run it

Publish directly and invite other students as editors. You review their drafts before publication.

The standards still apply

Keep coverage nonpartisan, link to sources, and avoid endorsements. MDCW can suspend sites that do not meet these standards.

Take action locally

Have your say

Read the agenda, watch a meeting, or share your views with commissioners.

  1. 01

    Read the agenda

    Full BCC and committee agendas are published in advance through the county's legislative system. Our Agenda Summaries explain each item in plain language, but the official agenda is the complete record.

    Browse legislation ↗

  2. 02

    Speak at public comment

    Members of the public can address the Board on agenda items. You can attend the meeting in person, or, when listed on the meeting notice, leave a voice message ahead of time on the phone number provided for that meeting.

    Students: come with the Commission Watch team. Sign up for the next meeting, or see photos from past meetings.

  3. 03

    Watch a meeting

    Meetings are webcast when available, so you can follow along without attending in person.

    Watch meetings ↗

  4. 04

    Contact your commissioner

    Call or email your commissioner’s office to share a concern or request a meeting.

    Commissioner directory

  5. 05

    Follow along between meetings

    We publish a Meeting Summary after each meeting we cover. Check back there, or reach out if you'd like to be notified.

    Read student editorials

This page describes the general public-participation process as published by Miami-Dade County. Specific procedures (sign-up deadlines, comment time limits, phone numbers) can vary by meeting, so always check the notice for the specific meeting you plan to attend or comment on.
