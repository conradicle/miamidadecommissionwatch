Editorials

Student perspectives on county government

Opinion pieces written by Miami-Dade County students about the Board of County Commissioners, constitutional offices, and county legislation. Unlike the rest of this site, editorials are opinion, not nonpartisan reporting — and they belong to their authors, not to us.

Submit your editorial ↗
These are opinions, not our reporting. Every editorial below is written and submitted by a Miami-Dade County student and is credited by name and school. It reflects that student's own views, not the views of Miami-Dade Commission Watch. Our news and meeting coverage remains nonpartisan; this section is the deliberate exception. Editorials are reviewed before publishing but are not fact-checked line by line the way our meeting summaries are — see our editorial policy.

Miami-Dade Commission Watch is an independent, student-run project founded by Conrad Gelber and Daniel Llobet. It is not affiliated with, operated by, or endorsed by Miami-Dade County government. All information is sourced from public records; see the official Board of County Commissioners site linked above for authoritative agendas and legislation.

Spanish and Haitian Creole translations on this site are generated automatically by Google Translate and are not reviewed by us. They may contain errors, especially for legal and government terms. When in doubt, refer to the English original.
