Part I

Executive Summary

The Board of County Commissioners meets Tuesday, October 20, 2026 at the Stephen P. Clark Government Center. This summary covers five agenda items eligible for public comment. Two involve long-term use of county land: a fast-tracked lease of 5.85 acres on West Flagler Street for affordable housing, and an extension of the Metro Grande ground lease from 75 to 99 years. The Board will also consider reappointing Inspector General Felix Jimenez, ratifying $213.7 million in contracts already awarded or started, and settling lawsuits brought by the billboard company Road.Space Media.

Every item description in this summary is drawn from the official agenda. MDCW does not take positions on agenda items.

Item 11A5 · Sponsor: Chairman Rodriguez · Resolution

Fast-tracked lease of county land for affordable housing

Site9250 W Flagler St.
Area5.85 acres
UseAffordable housing
ProcessExpedited

Item 11A5 authorizes a lease of county-owned land at 9250 W Flagler Street for an affordable housing development. The resolution waives the standard auditor review and public outreach requirements that normally apply to leases of county land, and the agenda itself describes the item as fast-tracked.

What it does
