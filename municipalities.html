How it works

You get your own site

Your city gets its own address — yourcity.miamidadecommissionwatch.com — with its own officials directory, meeting summaries, map and public comment guide.

You run it

You publish directly. Nothing waits on us. You can invite other students as editors, and their drafts come to you rather than to us.

The standards still apply

Nonpartisan, sourced, no endorsements. Link the record for every claim. We can suspend a city that stops meeting that bar, which is the only thing we hold back.
