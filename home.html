Why this exists

Why we do this

County Commission decisions affect housing, roads, and public services. Long agendas and unfamiliar terms can make them hard to follow. We help you understand the decisions and take part.

We explain the decisions and link to the public record so you can form your own view.

Miami-Dade Commission Watch is an independent, student-run project founded by Conrad Gelber and Daniel Llobet. It is not affiliated with, operated by, or endorsed by Miami-Dade County government or any municipal government. All information is sourced from public records; see the official government sites linked above for authoritative agendas and legislation.

Spanish and Haitian Creole translations on this site are generated automatically by Google Translate and are not reviewed by us. They may contain errors, especially for legal and government terms. When in doubt, refer to the English original.
