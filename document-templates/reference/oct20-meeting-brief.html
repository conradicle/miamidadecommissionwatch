Section 1

Executive Summary

The Board of County Commissioners meets Tuesday, October 20, 2026 at the Stephen P. Clark Government Center. This brief covers five agenda items eligible for public comment. Two involve long-term use of county land: a fast-tracked lease of 5.85 acres on West Flagler Street for affordable housing, and an extension of the Metro Grande ground lease from 75 to 99 years. The Board will also consider reappointing Inspector General Felix Jimenez, ratifying $213.7 million in contracts already awarded or started, and settling lawsuits brought by the billboard company Road.Space Media.

Every item description in this brief is drawn from the official agenda. MDCW does not take positions on agenda items.

Key Issues by Item

01
Item 11A5 · Sponsor: Chairman Rodriguez · Resolution

Fast-tracked lease of county land for affordable housing

Site9250 W Flagler St.
Area5.85 acres
UseAffordable housing
ProcessExpedited

What it does

Authorizes a lease of county-owned land at 9250 W Flagler Street for an affordable housing development.

Key issues

  • The item waives the standard auditor review and public outreach requirements that normally apply to leases of county land.
  • The agenda itself describes the item as fast-tracked.

Reappointment of Inspector General Felix Jimenez

OfficeInspector General
TermFour years

What it does

Reappoints Felix Jimenez as Inspector General for a new four-year term and approves his contract.

Background

The Office of the Inspector General investigates fraud, waste and misconduct in county government.

Current term75 years

Extends the developer's ground lease on county land from 75 years to 99 years.

04
Items 8O1 + 8N2 · Ratification

Ratification of major contracts

8O1$184.3M · Poole & Kent
8N2$29.4M · Horsepower Electric
Combined$213.7M

What it does

Asks the Board to formally approve two contracts that have already been awarded or started: a $184.3 million pump station at the North District Wastewater Treatment Plant built by Poole & Kent, and a $29.4 million two-year emergency streetlighting contract with Horsepower Electric.

Background

Ratification is the Board's formal approval of a contract after it has been awarded or work has begun.

Item 11A7 settles lawsuits brought against the county by billboard company Road.Space Media. Linked ordinance 4N would allow variances from sign spacing and height rules tied to annexation and incorporation applications.
