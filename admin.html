Photos from Board meetings, shown on /photos, under that meeting's Meeting Summary, and (featured ones) on the homepage and /attend. Featured photos rotate at the top of the homepage, the first thing visitors see, so feature wide shots of the chamber or the group rather than close-ups of a student's face. Most people in these photos are minors: publish a photo only if a signed photo release is on file for every minor in it, and leave out anyone who asked not to be posted. Captions use first name and last initial at most, never a school or grade. Uploads are resized in your browser and their location data is removed before anything is stored.
