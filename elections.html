Elections

Every office Miami-Dade voters decide

The Mayor, the 13-member Board of County Commissioners, and the county's constitutional officers are all elected directly by voters, on different schedules. Here's what's on the ballot in 2026, when everything else is next up, and how to register and vote.

Key dates

2026 election calendar

Primary Election

Tuesday, August 18, 2026. Polls open 7:00 a.m.–7:00 p.m. Early voting runs August 3–16. Vote-by-mail ballots must be received by August 6.

General Election

Tuesday, November 3, 2026. Voter registration deadline is October 5. Vote-by-mail ballots must be received by October 22.

Registration deadline (Primary)

Monday, July 20, 2026. Applications postmarked or received by an official voter registration agency by this date count for the primary.

Many countywide and county commission races in Miami-Dade are decided outright in the August primary if one candidate wins a majority of the vote — there's no November runoff in that case. Always check the official 2026 election calendar for the specific dates and deadlines that apply to your race.
2026 races

What's on the ballot this year

County Commission, even districts

Districts 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, and 12 are up for election in 2026 under the charter's staggered schedule. Districts 1, 3, 5, 7, 9, 11, and 13 were last elected in 2024 and are next up in 2028.

District 1, special election

District 1 also appears on the 2026 ballot in a special election to fill the remainder of the term, following the sitting commissioner's planned resignation effective January 3, 2027.

Miami-Dade County School Board

Several School Board seats are also on the 2026 ballot. School Board races aren't covered in detail on this site yet; see the county's official candidate information for specifics.

The Mayor and the seven constitutional officers (Property Appraiser, Tax Collector, Clerk of the Courts & Comptroller, Supervisor of Elections, Sheriff, State Attorney, and Public Defender) are not on the 2026 ballot — all were elected to four-year terms in 2024 and are next up in 2028. Voter guide

How to register and vote

Register to vote

Register online, by mail, or in person through the Supervisor of Elections. You must be a U.S. citizen, Florida resident, and at least 18 by Election Day.

Register with the Supervisor of Elections ↗

Check your registration

Confirm you're registered, see your assigned precinct, and find your polling place.

Voter information lookup ↗

Vote by mail

Request a vote-by-mail ballot in advance, or drop it off at an official secure drop box or early voting site before the deadline for that election.

Vote early or in person

Early voting sites are open countywide in the days before each election; on Election Day, you vote at your assigned precinct.
