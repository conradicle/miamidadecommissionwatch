School Ambassadors

Bring your school to County Commission meetings

MDCW School Ambassadors help students at their school attend Miami-Dade County Commission meetings. If you want to get your classmates involved in local government, apply below.

What ambassadors do

Each ambassador represents one school. You will:

  1. Recruit classmates to come to Board of County Commissioners meetings.
  2. Find out how your school excuses absences for these meetings, and help your group follow those steps.
  3. Organize rides so your school's group can get to the meeting.
  4. Lead your school's group on meeting day.

What you get

Ambassadors earn community service hours and the title MDCW School Ambassador. You report to MDCW's Executive Director, who supports you along the way.

Applying does not make you an ambassador right away. MDCW's co-founders read every application and reach out by email. We pick one ambassador per school.
