Speak Out September

Six ways to make your voice heard at the County Commission this September

Speak Out September is a civic participation campaign from Virtutem Populo, encouraging students and residents to engage with their county government in six concrete ways over the course of the month: Speak, Write, Meet, Research, Create, and Organize. This page shows how Miami-Dade Commission Watch's tools support each one.

Miami-Dade Commission Watch is an independent, nonpartisan project. We don't take positions on legislation or endorse campaigns, candidates, or organizations. This page simply maps our existing tools — the district finder, meeting tracker, officials directory, and editorial platform — to the six actions Speak Out September asks people to take. Nothing here reflects an opinion on any specific issue before the Board.

How this site supports each one

Speak

Public comment at a Board of County Commissioners meeting is open to any resident on agenda items up for a vote. Find your district above, then see when the Board meets this September.

Write

A letter to a commissioner's office, testimony submitted for the record, an op-ed, or a student editorial are all ways to put your position on a county issue in writing.

Meet

Commissioner offices take meeting requests from constituents in their district. Start from the directory to find contact information for your commissioner's office.
