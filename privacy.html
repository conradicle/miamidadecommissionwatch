Privacy & Terms

Privacy Policy & Terms of Use

Plain-language version: we collect the minimum needed to run the site and review editorial submissions, we don't sell or share it, and you can ask us to correct or delete what we have. Details below.

Last updated August 17, 2026.

Privacy Policy

What we collect

If you submit an editorial: your full name, grade, school name, school email address, and the text of your editorial, through the form on Submit an Editorial.

If you're staff: your email address and login credentials, managed through our authentication provider (Supabase), for the admin dashboard used to review submissions and publish content.

If you use the district finder: the address you type is sent to the U.S. Census Bureau's public geocoding service through our server, so we can tell you which commission district it falls in. We don't store the addresses you look up.

General site use: we don't run analytics or advertising trackers. Your browser may store a small amount of local data (like your chosen site language) that stays on your device.

Why we collect it

Name, grade, school, and email on editorial submissions let us verify you're a Miami-Dade County high school student before anything is reviewed or published, and let us follow up with you about your draft. If your editorial is published, your name, grade, and school are shown publicly alongside it, exactly as submitted — your email address is never published or shared; we use it only to contact you about your submission.

Who can see it

Unpublished and rejected submissions are visible only to Miami-Dade Commission Watch's founders and any staff we've given admin access to review submissions. We don't sell, rent, or share your information with any third party for marketing purposes.

Where it's stored

Editorial submissions and staff accounts are stored with Supabase, our database provider. The site itself is hosted on Vercel. Both are reputable providers used broadly across the industry; neither has any right to use your data beyond storing and serving it on our behalf.

How long we keep it

If your editorial is published, we keep the published version (name, grade, school, and text) as part of the public record, the same way a newspaper keeps its archives. If it's not published — whether rejected or simply never reviewed — we keep it only as long as needed for our review process, and delete it on request (see below).

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete any information we hold about you, including a published editorial, by emailing contact@miamidadecommissionwatch.com. We'll respond within a reasonable time and confirm once it's done.

Children's privacy

Miami-Dade Commission Watch's editorial submission form is intended for high school students in grades 9–12 only, and the form itself only accepts those grade levels. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that we've unintentionally collected information from a child under 13, we'll delete it promptly — contact us at the email above if you believe this has happened.

Security

We use industry-standard practices to protect the data we hold, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and access controls that limit who can see submitted content before it's published. No website can guarantee perfect security, but we take reasonable steps appropriate to a small civic organization holding a modest amount of information.

Translations

Spanish and Haitian Creole translations on this site are generated automatically by Google Translate and aren't reviewed by us. When in doubt, refer to the English original, including for this policy.

Changes to this policy

If we materially change what we collect or how we use it, we'll update this page and change the "last updated" date above.

Terms of Use

What this site is

Miami-Dade Commission Watch is an independent, student-run project. It is not affiliated with, operated by, or endorsed by Miami-Dade County government. Content is provided for informational and civic-education purposes and isn't a substitute for the county's own official records, which are linked throughout the site.

Accuracy

We source every factual claim and try hard to get things right, but we're students, not a newsroom with a copy desk. Information may occasionally be outdated or contain errors. If you spot one, please let us know and we'll correct it.

Submitted content

By submitting an editorial, you confirm it's your own original work and grant Miami-Dade Commission Watch a non-exclusive license to publish, edit for length and clarity, and display it on this site with your name, grade, and school attached. You retain ownership of your writing. Editorials represent the views of their individual authors, not Miami-Dade Commission Watch.

Acceptable use

Don't use this site to submit false information, impersonate someone else, or attempt to disrupt the site's operation (including automated scraping of submission forms or attempts to access non-public data).

No warranty

This site is provided "as is," without warranties of any kind, to the extent permitted by law.

Contact

Questions about these terms or our privacy practices: contact@miamidadecommissionwatch.com.
