Cities

Start your city's Commission Watch

Live in Miami Beach, Hialeah, Doral — anywhere in Miami-Dade with its own city government? Apply to lead student coverage of it. We review every application, and approved cities go live on this site with you running them.

How it works

Who can apply

Any high school student (grades 9–12) enrolled at a Miami-Dade County public, charter, private, or home school. You should live in or attend school in the city you want to cover. Use your school-issued email so we can verify enrollment.

What you'd do

Read your city's meeting agendas, summarize what its government discussed and decided in plain language, and keep its page here current — the same thing we do for county government, scaled to your city.

Approval, not auto-publish

Applications go to our founders for review. We'll follow up by email, usually to talk before deciding. If approved, your city's page switches on with you listed as its founding editor.

Same nonpartisan rules

City coverage follows the site's standards: no endorsements, no spin, and a link to the original source for every claim. You'd be independently monitoring your city's government — never speaking for it.

Your privacy

Public pages credit you by first name and last initial only — never your full surname, grade, school, or email. Your application is visible only to our staff.

No coding needed

You do the journalism; the website side is handled. If you can read an agenda and write clearly, you're qualified.

Application

Submitting sends your application directly to our review queue. Only staff can see it; nothing about you is published unless your city is approved, and then only your first name and last initial.
