Editorials

Submit an editorial

Miami-Dade students: share your opinion on a county decision. Check the guidelines below, then send us your draft.

Read the guidelinesGo to submission form

Guidelines

Who can submit

Any high school student (grades 9–12) enrolled at a Miami-Dade County public or charter school. The form needs your MDCPS student email — the one ending in @students.dadeschools.net — so we can verify enrollment before publishing. If you attend a private or home school, we still want your writing: email it to contact@miamidadecommissionwatch.com and we'll take it that way. We're not able to accept submissions from middle school or elementary students.

What to write about

Your own opinion on something the Board of County Commissioners, the Mayor, or one of the county's constitutional offices has done or is considering. Check our Meeting and Agenda Summaries and Constitutional Offices pages for ideas.

Length

Aim for 400–800 words. We may suggest edits for length or clarity.

Original & your own

Submissions must be your own original writing, not AI-generated, and not previously published elsewhere. Cite any facts or figures you use.

Review before publication

We read every submission and follow up before anything goes live. We may suggest edits, ask clarifying questions, or decline to publish. Submitting a draft doesn't guarantee it'll be posted.

It's your opinion, not ours

Published editorials are bylined with your first name and last initial only — never your full surname, grade, school, or email — and represent your views alone. Miami-Dade Commission Watch's own news and meeting coverage stays nonpartisan; editorials are the one section where that changes, on purpose.

Submission form

Only staff reviewing submissions can see your draft. Nothing is published until it is approved.
