Official documents

Meeting and Agenda Summaries

For each regular meeting of the Board of County Commissioners, Miami-Dade Commission Watch publishes two documents: an Agenda Summary before the meeting and a Meeting Summary after it. Both are nonpartisan and drawn from primary sources. Each one is a web page you can read here and an official PDF you can download.

Before each meeting

Agenda Summaries

An in-depth guide to the agenda, about 60 pages, that works as a readable alternative to the county's official agenda: the backstory, legislative history, fiscal impact and a plain-language explanation of each item.

After each meeting

Meeting Summaries

What happened at the meeting: which items passed, failed, were deferred or withdrawn, the vote counts, and the major news from the meeting.

Looking ahead

Upcoming regular meetings

Regular meetings are open to public comment. Dates and times come from the county's official 2026 Meeting Calendar (PDF); budget hearings, zoning meetings and committee meetings are not listed here. An Agenda Summary is posted before each meeting once the county publishes its agenda.

About these documents

Numbers, versions and corrections

Document numbers

Every document has a number that names its type and meeting date. MDCW-AS-2026-1020 is the Agenda Summary for the meeting of October 20, 2026; MDCW-MS-2026-1020 is the Meeting Summary for the same meeting. Cite the number when you refer to a document.

Corrections

When we correct a document we publish a new version (1.0, then 1.1) and keep every earlier version available to download. The document page says when it was updated, and its version history lists what changed. Report an error.

The earlier tracker

Before these documents, we summarized meetings item by item in the Meeting Tracker. It stays online, unchanged, as the Meeting Tracker Archive for meetings through October 2026.
